# Lunar Frontier: The Last 30 Days — Backlog (V2+ Scope Parking Lot)

The following ideas and features are out of scope for V1 and are recorded here to prevent scope creep:

1. **Multiplayer & Co-op Command**: Multiple commanders managing different sectors of the lunar outpost simultaneously.
2. **Procedural Terrain & Infinite Map Generation**: Dynamic generation of non-South Pole lunar terrain beyond the curated Shackleton / Malapert mission area.
3. **Modding & Custom Scripting Support**: Lua / JSON mod loader for community buildings and missions.
4. **Additional Crew Expansion**: Expanding beyond the 4 founding crew members to support 12+ astronauts.
5. **Full Voice Acting**: Audio voice tracks for all dialogue and mission-control advisories (placeholder text and Web Audio synthesizers are used in V1).
6. **Mobile Touch & Gyro Controls**: Dedicated mobile virtual joysticks and touch UI adapters.
7. **Lunar Sub-surface Lava Tube Exploration**: Underground procedural cave systems and bases.
